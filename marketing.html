Ride · Build · Defend

A cozy castle defense for iPhone and iPad. Ride out on your white horse, raise towers and walls, grow a town — and hold the realm against wave after wave of goblins.

Get it on the App Store
Why you'll love it
🏹

One finger, one knight

Steer with a floating joystick anywhere on the screen. Your knight shoots on his own — you choose where to ride and who to save.

🏰

Build by riding

Stop on a plot and watch the coins flow in. Archer towers, palisades, a forge, farms, a market, a temple — and every level of every building looks different.

⚔️

Goblins, orcs & warlords

Raids come down the forest roads: shielded orcs you must ride around, rams that go straight for your walls, and warlords who will not leave quietly.

📜

A chronicle with a smile

Your steward, Captain Brenna and a kingdom of villagers bring you news, troubles and choices — hire Sir Baldric the Not-Quite-Brave, or send him off?

🪿

Unlikely allies

A war dog that bites, a goat that headbutts goblins off their feet, and a goose that picks one goblin and chases it to the bitter end.

🛡️

Play your way

Fully offline, no account, no ads that interrupt you — reward videos only when you choose one. In English, German, French, Ukrainian, Spanish, Japanese and Portuguese.
